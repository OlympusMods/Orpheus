# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Forge and NeoForge: use `iconFile` in the mod metadata instead of the deprecated `logoFile`

## 1.0.0

- Separate song lists, timing and music source for the main menu, survival, creative, underwater, the Nether, the End, the Ender Dragon fight and the credits
- Song chance from 1 to 10, shortest and longest pause between songs, and a rule against repeating the last songs
- Music discs can join the normal rotation
- Keybinds to open the menu, go to the previous song, skip, pause or resume, and block the current song
- Now Playing pop-up with the song and artist
- Presets to save, copy and switch whole setups
- Music Options button on the Options screen
- Downloads songs your game is missing from Mojang's servers into a resource pack
- Unused album tracks play from your own .ogg files
- Fabric, Forge and NeoForge for Minecraft 1.20 to 26.3
